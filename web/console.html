ACCOUNT FUNDSUSDC · Escrow · seller + buyer share one pot

Every connected wallet has one escrow pot. AVAILABLE is the free balance only — locked payments are not included, and this is not seller revenue (a seller's earnings land here too). DEPOSIT tops the pot up — buyer locks draw from it and leftovers return here; the contract holds the funds, never a seller. WITHDRAW moves available USDC back to your wallet.

SHARED RELAY CUSTODYplanned shared mode · the key is sealed in your browser to the pinned enclave key

One trusted relay serves many sellers. VERIFY checks the live relay against the deployment pins (origin · signer · upload key · app id · report data), then your upstream key is encrypted in this browser to the pinned TEE key and submitted — the relay stores it inside the enclave and probes your live model catalog with it. The key is never written to page storage, logs, or the URL — but JavaScript cannot guarantee a secure wipe of memory, so close this tab when you are done.
