POLICY

Platform Policy & Risk Notice. Six short sections — what this is, what can go wrong, and exactly where the responsibility sits. Read it before you sell or buy.

MONAD TESTNET · 10143
01

WHAT THIS ISthe prototype, in one paragraph

TokenShare is a decentralized marketplace prototype that lets sellers rent out unused LLM API quota per request. Buyers lock USDC in an on-chain escrow contract (Monad testnet); sellers are paid by cryptographically verified usage; unused funds are refundable on-chain after the lock TTL.

02

TESTNET ONLY — NO REAL FUNDSchain ID 10143

This deployment runs entirely on Monad testnet (chain ID 10143) with test USDC. Tokens here have no real-world value. Never send mainnet funds to any contract or address shown in this app.

03

UPSTREAM PROVIDER TERMS — SELLER RISKsellers: read twice

Sellers share access to their own LLM provider subscriptions (e.g. Kimi, OpenCode Zen). Many providers' Terms of Service prohibit reselling, sharing, or redistributing account access. Selling quota through TokenShare may result in suspension or termination of the seller's upstream account. Sellers act at their own risk. TokenShare does not mediate, endorse, or take responsibility for any upstream ToS violation.

04

NO CUSTODY OF KEYSkeys never leave the seller's relay

TokenShare never holds, stores, or transmits seller API keys. Keys live only inside the seller's own relay — optionally inside a Trusted Execution Environment (TEE), where they are hardware-sealed.

05

TRUST MODELverify, don't trust

Usage is reported by the seller's relay and billed per token. Every charge is backed by an EIP-712 signed receipt verifiable by anyone. Buyer funds are protected by the escrow contract: disputed or undelivered usage stays locked and becomes refundable after expiry.

06

NO WARRANTYas-is, hackathon build

This is a hackathon prototype provided strictly as-is, with no warranty of any kind. No SLA, no uptime guarantee, no liability for loss of funds, accounts, or data. Use at your own risk.
